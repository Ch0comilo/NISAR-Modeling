# Radar interpretation for the science demo

| Output | What it shows and what can explain it | Support and limitations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct · original power and signed dB | HH/VV returns vary with roughness, dielectric properties and geometry. HV/VH responds to depolarizing scattering such as vegetation or structure. A drop can motivate a canopy-change hypothesis. | Use actual polarization, comparator and dates. Moisture, freeze/thaw, tillage, viewing geometry and noise are alternatives. [ASF GCOV](https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/gcov/) |
| Translatable · polarization RGB and ratio | Colors mix named returns and their balance. A ratio rise can result from cross-pol brightening, co-pol darkening or both. | Inspect component ranges and original channels. Colors cannot isolate a material or scattering mechanism. [Visualization contract](API.md#radar-visualizations) |
| Translatable · temporal RGB, median and MAD | Actual-date colors compare returns, and MAD describes variability over the selected acquisitions. | Inspect sampling and support. MAD measures temporal spread. Instrument noise is unestimated. [Visualization contract](API.md#radar-visualizations) |
| Translatable · flood, fire, vegetation and agriculture | Smooth exposed water can darken. Wind or vegetation can alter the sign. Repeated HV decline can motivate a structural-change hypothesis. Focal change relative to nearby cells can motivate a management hypothesis. | Compare with optical snapshots, field records and acquisition context. Qadirabad's marker and area can give different answers. [Flood characterization](FLOOD_CHARACTERIZATION.md) · [Geographic holdout](EXPLORATION_HOLDOUT.md) |
| Direct · coherence and phase | Coherence measures interferometric consistency. Changes in scatterers, temporal baseline and viewing geometry can reduce it. Wrapped phase repeats every phase cycle. | In Kumamoto, only 3 of 1,614 common phase cells met correction-input checks. Atmosphere, ionosphere, unwrapping and reference quality matter. Validated LOS stays null. [InSAR report](MODELING_V2.md) · [ASF GUNW](https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/gunw/) |
| Translatable · GP and spectra | GP is a conditional historical fit. Candidate periods depend on sampling and tested frequency range. | Spatial quartiles, conditional block intervals, GP model spread and instrument noise are distinct. Held-out GP checks apply to observation reconstruction on the named regions. [V2 benchmark](MODELING_V2.md) |
| Opaque · learned RGB and UMAP | A fitted representation can help select observed trajectories for inspection. | No mechanism or event class is assigned to a color or group. V2 loses to interpolation on both new geographic reconstruction tests. [Model card](../models/global_explorer_v2/MODEL_CARD.md) · [UMAP report](REPRESENTATION_UMAP.md) |

More compatible L-band histories could support wetland persistence and field-change studies with independent context. Corrected and geodetically checked GUNW networks could support deformation studies. L/S comparisons require matched measurements and calibration.
